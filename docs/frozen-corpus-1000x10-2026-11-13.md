# Frozen 1,000-source / 10,000-input corpus

The acquisition requirement is materialized, not just indexed. The final local
manifest is `benchmarks/multires-combined/frozen-1000x10-20261113.json`, SHA-256
`18945176cd1c70426c1d78f92cde635d6e2411a30d6f1abb2c191850f51a5580`.
It contains 1,000 distinct qualified STEP sources, each with ten different STL
geometry hashes. This establishes fixtures; it is **not a completed conversion
baseline or a reconstruction success rate**. Geometry and private evidence stay
outside Git.

The fixed composition is 980 Fusion 360 Gallery models and 20 public KiCad
models. Acquisition produced 1,000 usable primary models after 382 exclusions
and 22 usable KiCad models after nine exclusions. The extra qualified models
are retained replacements. Individual exclusion records, provenance, exact
exporter versions, source/mesh hashes and materialization witnesses remain in
the local manifests and records. The final assembler rechecked actual source
licenses and every selected case against its materialization record.

Fusion inputs use the Gallery Dataset's non-commercial research license.
KiCad sources are pinned to `b8b3cfdfad88ba66f21002b3de51dc6f7d55ba5a`,
CC-BY-SA 4.0 with its KiCad exception. Source index Git blob identities and
license bytes are verified. No private model was uploaded or paid data used.
See [acquisition and composition](multires-fixture-witnesses.md) for the
resumable tools and witness checks.

All exports use millimetres, absolute linear tolerances and serial OCCT
tessellation, with cached triangulation cleared between presets. Exporter
versions are CadQuery 2.8.0, cadquery-ocp 7.9.3.1.1 and trimesh 4.12.2.

Each preset has exactly 1,000 actual files. Exact asset and conservative native
geometry signatures are deduplicated before selection. The 1,000 sources belong
to 859 declared geometry families. All ten variants and all members of a family
stay in one split:

| Split | Source models | Geometry families | STL cases |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 666 | 570 | 6,660 |
| Validation | 165 | 143 | 1,650 |
| Holdout | 169 | 146 | 1,690 |

Previously exposed historical models are development-only. Holdout conversion
failures are not used to tune individual experiments. Ten exports are correlated
tests of one design, not ten independent designs. Personal difficult models are
separate private regression anchors and are not included in these counts.

There are 888 models containing cylinders, 139 cones, 224 B-spline surfaces,
192 tori and 39 spheres; categories overlap. There are 998 single-solid models
and two two-solid models. This is biased toward Gallery parts and smaller
electronic/mechanical components, with weak multi-solid coverage. Requiring ten
distinct tessellations excludes purely planar models whose exports are identical.
The corpus cannot establish equal reliability for arbitrary assemblies, very
large scans or the exact original feature history.

Reproduce the final assembly with a new output filename, preserving this freeze:

```powershell
python tools/assemble_multires_corpus.py `
  --collection benchmarks/multires-1000/manifest.json 980 `
  --collection benchmarks/kicad-step-b8b3cfdf/fixtures/manifest.json 20 `
  --source-index benchmarks/kicad-step-b8b3cfdf/source-index.json `
  --license-file '<authorized Fusion dataset>/LICENSE.md' `
  --output benchmarks/multires-combined/new-verification.json --freeze
```

The complete quantitative baseline still requires resumable isolated conversions
and frozen ground-truth grading. Fixture-readiness errors are acquisition errors,
not converter failures, and require recorded replacements before claiming a
complete 1,000-source baseline. Progressively evaluate small discriminating
cohorts first, then justified regression and full-corpus batches against these
same input/settings identities. Existing strict gates and deadlines are unchanged.
